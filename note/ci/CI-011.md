`verify_frama_c` runs `frama-c -version` and `script/verify_frama_c.sh` on `$FRAMA_C_IMAGE`,
`framac/frama-c:32.1`: every row of `verification/frama-c/expectation.txt` must prove all its
goals with the expected count (WP-007). The local opam switch pins the same Frama-C
(`opam pin add frama-c 32.1`, PROVER-002), so local and CI goal counts agree. Move the image tag
and the local pin together; do not make the job `allow_failure` to paper over a mismatch.

- `DIANA_JOB` is unset in CI, so `script/job_count.sh` gives the runner's `nproc` and that many
  rows run at a time.
- The job waits for `test_gcc_release` (optional need, no artifacts) and keeps
  `verification/frama-c/report.txt` a month, also when it fails (`when: always`).
- Path list `.change_frama_c` (CI-002): `include/`, `src/`, `verification/frama-c/`, the three
  Frama-C scripts and `script/job_count.sh`, which `script/verify_frama_c.sh` calls.

The repository does not record the Alt-Ergo version inside the image; the local pin is 2.6.4.
