`verify_lean` (image `$LEAN_IMAGE`, `needs: []`, timeout 2 h) runs `lake exe cache get && lake
build` in `verification/lean`, then `python3 script/check_spec.py --run --strict --prover lean
--no-report`. It fails when the Lean check does not run, a listed Lean theorem is missing, or a
theorem depends on an axiom outside `script/axiom_allow.yaml`; `--prover lean` leaves out the
Rocq-only layers and the Frama-C expectation check.

The gate runs under the image's system `python3` with `python3-yaml`, not under uv. On Debian
bookworm that is Python 3.11, hence `per-file-target-version` `script/** = py311` for ruff in
`pyproject.toml` (STYLE-009). `LEAN_IMAGE` is the stock `debian:bookworm-slim` until the image of
`ci/docker/lean.Dockerfile` (CI-015) is in a registry; `before_script` works on both:

- `elan` not on `PATH` (stock image): apt-installs `curl git ca-certificates zstd python3
  python3-yaml`, installs elan into `$CI_PROJECT_DIR/.cache/elan` (`ELAN_HOME`) with the
  toolchain of `verification/lean/lean-toolchain`, and prepends its `bin` to `PATH`;
- `elan` on `PATH` (project image): prints its version and uses the image's toolchain;
- then, when `DIANA_LEAN_CACHE` is set and `verification/lean/.lake/packages` is absent, it
  copies `$DIANA_LEAN_CACHE/.lake/packages` there, so `lake exe cache get` neither clones Mathlib
  nor downloads its build.

The job cache (key `lean-$CI_COMMIT_REF_SLUG`) keeps `.cache/elan` and
`verification/lean/.lake/build`. Path list `.change_lean`: `verification/lean/`, `spec/`,
`script/check_spec.py`, `script/axiom_allow.yaml` and `ci/docker/lean.Dockerfile`.
