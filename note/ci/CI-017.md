---
id: CI-017
title: GitHub Actions mirror the GitLab pipeline and its needs graph in ci.yml
kind: fact
path:
  - .github/workflows/ci.yml
  - .github/workflows/c_test.yml
  - .github/workflows/style_git.yml
related: [CI-001, CI-014, CI-018, CI-019, CI-023, CI-026]
---
GitLab CI is the primary pipeline. The GitHub repository is a push mirror of the GitLab project,
so every push runs `.github/workflows/` as a second pipeline over the same commits: `ci.yml` holds
every GitLab job but `style_git` and `release`, under the GitLab names, and `style_git.yml` holds
`style_git`, apart because it must never be cancelled (CI-019). The job `change` decides which jobs
run (CI-018); the `needs` of `.gitlab-ci.yml` (CI-001) become `needs:` in `ci.yml`:

- `test_{gcc,clang}_{debug,release}`, each a GitLab `build_*` and `test_*` pair in one call of the
  reusable `c_test.yml`, and the macOS `test_macos_{debug,release}` need `style_c`;
- `test_install` and `verify_frama_c` need `test_gcc_release`, `coverage_c` `test_gcc_debug`,
  `coverage_python` `test_python` (all three legs, CI-024), and `test_install_macos` `test_macos`;
- the style jobs, `test_python`, `test_python_macos` and `verify_{lean,rocq,provenance}` need only
  `change`.

GitLab's `needs` with `optional: true` becomes `if: !failure() && !cancelled() && <list>`: a needed
job that its path list skipped does not block, one that failed does. GitHub cannot make a job wait
for one in another workflow, hence one workflow. Triggers: a push to any branch, a tag matching
`v[0-9]+.[0-9]+.[0-9]+` (matched against the whole name, so `v1.0.0-rc1` does not), and
`workflow_dispatch`; unlike GitLab, a branch push without a merge request runs, since a mirrored
branch has none. `ci.yml` has the concurrency group `ci-<ref>` with `cancel-in-progress`, as
`interruptible` and GitLab's auto-cancel. Both workflows have `permissions: contents: read`, no
secret and no release, publishing or deployment job (CI-014). Timeouts: `change` 10 min,
`verify_lean` 120 min, every other job 60 min; no retry on runner failure.
