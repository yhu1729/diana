---
id: CI-025
title: Update the pinned actions and image digests of the GitHub workflows
kind: procedure
path:
  - .github/workflows/*.yml
related: [CI-011, CI-017, CI-020]
---
Every third-party action is pinned to a full commit SHA with its tag in a comment
(`actions/checkout@<sha> # v7.0.1`), and every image to GitLab's tag plus a digest
(`gcc:14@sha256:<digest>`). GitLab follows its tags, so the GitHub images stay frozen while
GitLab's move; refresh them, and move a tag in both files at once.

1. Action: choose a release at least two weeks old and resolve its commit with
   `git ls-remote https://github.com/<owner>/<repo> 'refs/tags/<tag>' 'refs/tags/<tag>^{}'`; the
   `^{}` line, when there is one, is the commit of an annotated tag. Replace the SHA and the
   comment in every workflow, and check the inputs the workflows pass against the new
   `action.yml`.
2. Image: keep the tag of `.gitlab-ci.yml` and take the digest of its manifest list, for example
   the `Digest:` line of `docker buildx imagetools inspect <image>:<tag>`, and replace it in every
   workflow that names the image. A new `framac/frama-c` tag also moves the local opam pin
   (CI-011), and a new uv image version a leg of CI-024.
3. Run `actionlint` with `shellcheck` on `.github/workflows/*.yml` (`uvx --from actionlint-py
   --with shellcheck-py actionlint .github/workflows/*.yml` provides both), then push a branch to
   GitLab and check the mirror's runs.
