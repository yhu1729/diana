---
id: CI-026
title: GitHub caches uv, Lean and the WP cache under keys of their inputs only
kind: fact
path:
  - .github/workflows/ci.yml
  - uv.lock
  - verification/lean/lean-toolchain
  - verification/lean/lake-manifest.json
  - verification/frama-c/expectation.txt
related: [CI-017, CI-022, WP-009, WP-010]
---
A GitHub cache entry cannot be overwritten, is saved only when its key missed, and is visible to
its own branch and, from the default branch, to every branch. Every key in `ci.yml` is a hash of
the files the cached content depends on, never the commit, so an entry is saved once per change of
those files instead of on every run:

- uv, in `style_c`, `style_python`, `test_python` (per leg), `coverage_c` and `coverage_python`:
  `UV_CACHE_DIR=.cache/uv` under `uv-<job>[-<python>]-<hash of uv.lock>`, restoring the newest
  entry of the job when `uv.lock` changed; the cache keeps the downloaded wheels unpruned, since
  `uv cache prune --ci` would drop exactly those, every dependency here being a pre-built wheel;
  `test_python_macos` uses `setup-uv`'s own cache on `uv.lock`;
- Lean (CI-022): `.cache/elan` under the hash of `lean-toolchain`, `.lake/packages` (Mathlib and
  its compiled files) under the hash of `lean-toolchain` and `lake-manifest.json`, and
  `.lake/build` under that hash plus the hash of the Lean sources, restoring the newest build of
  the same toolchain and manifest; Lake tracks content hashes, so a restored build is reused;
- WP (`verify_frama_c`): `verification/frama-c/cache`, the per-row `-wp-cache update` directories
  (WP-009), under the hash of `src/`, `include/`, `expectation.txt` and `run_frama_c.sh`,
  restoring the newest when one changed; a verdict is keyed by its goal, so a changed goal is
  proved again. `actions/cache` saves only after a successful job, so a `Failed` verdict, which WP
  would replay (WP-010), never reaches the cache; beside it, the pinned Alt-Ergo binary (WP-013)
  under the hash of `script/fetch_alt_ergo.sh`.

Not cached: `verify_rocq`, whose `opam install` runs inside `docker run` and whose `make` rebuilds
any `.vo` older than its checked-out source; the Docker images, the apt packages and the C builds,
each a minute or less.
