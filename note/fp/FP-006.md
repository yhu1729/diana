`diana_fp_sqrt(x)` (`src/fp_sqrt.c`) returns `__builtin_sqrt(x)`, the one call of a compiler
builtin in Diana; `diana_cholesky` calls it once per pivot and `diana_norm_2` once, on a sum of
squares that is at least 1.

- Not a libm call (FP-001): gcc and clang compile the builtin to the IEEE square-root
  instruction, and CompCert's `clightgen` emits a call to its own `__builtin_sqrt`
  (`EF_builtin`, semantics `Float.sqrt`, which is Flocq's `Bsqrt`). With `errno` semantics gcc and
  Linux clang add a libm `sqrt` call for negative arguments, and gcc at `-O0` always calls it;
  `-fno-math-errno` (FP-004) removes it, so the library references no `sqrt` symbol.
- WP: the contract on the declaration in `src/fp_sqrt.h` (`requires \gt_double(x, 0.0)`; a finite
  `x` gives a finite result `> 0` equal to `(double)\sqrt(x)`; `+∞` gives `+∞`) is assumed at the
  call (`SKIP`, WP-003) and proved by CHOLESKY-B1. The IEEE comparison `\gt_double` is what the C
  test `!(s > 0.0)` establishes in `diana_cholesky`, and the loop invariant `\ge_double(s, 1.0)`
  in `diana_norm_2`; WP cannot prove the pivot finite.
- VST: `body_diana_fp_sqrt` (CHOLESKY-C2) proves that the body returns `Float.sqrt x`, the funspec
  that `VST/Cholesky.v` and `VST/Norm2.v` import, assuming
  for the builtin `builtin_sqrt_spec`, which returns `Float.sqrt x`: CompCert's semantics of
  `BI_fsqrt` (`Builtins0`). VST has no rule for a builtin call, so no `semax_body` discharges this
  funspec; it is the one assumed VST funspec in Diana (`doc/verification/trust.md`).
- The compiler comparison checks the gcc and clang builds bit for bit against `ccomp` on the
  corpus's Cholesky and 2-norm cases (COMPILER-006).
