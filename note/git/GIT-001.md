---
id: GIT-001
title: Commit subject is type(scope) summary, at most 50 characters, no article
kind: rule
path:
  - .git-hooks/commit-msg
  - script/check_commit.sh
related: [GIT-002, GIT-007, STYLE-001]
---
The subject is `<type>[(scope)]: <summary>`, with the type one of
`feat fix perf refactor build style test ci doc chore` (`doc`, not `docs`; no `!` marker), one
space after the colon, a summary that starts with a non-space character, at most 50 characters in
all, and no article `a`, `an` or `the` in the summary.

Name the thing, not the reason: `feat: add bisection header`, not
`feat: add the bisection reverse-communication header`. Drop articles and keep it short
(`build: rename DIANA_JOBS to DIANA_JOB`, `doc: describe parallel verify`). Rationale goes in
`doc/`, `note/` or `spec/`, never in the log.

Enforcement:

- the `commit-msg` hook checks only the `<type>[(scope)]: <summary>` form, at commit time;
- `script/check_commit.sh` (GIT-007) checks form, length and articles, in the `pre-push` hook and
  in CI `style_git`. Its article test matches only the lowercase words `a`, `an` and `the`
  bounded by spaces or the ends of the summary, so `The` or `a,` slip through; drop them anyway.
