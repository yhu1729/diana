---
id: GIT-002
title: Commit message is the subject and one Signed-off-by line, nothing else
kind: rule
path:
  - script/check_commit.sh
related: [GIT-001, GIT-007]
---
Commit with `git commit -s`. The message is the subject, a blank line, and exactly one
`Signed-off-by: <committer name> <committer email>` line, the trailer `-s` adds. No body and no
other trailer: no `Co-Authored-By`, no issue reference, no explanation. Explanations belong in
`doc/`, `note/` or `spec/`.

`script/check_commit.sh` enforces it: line 2 must be blank, and the non-blank lines after it must
be exactly `Signed-off-by: ` followed by the commit's committer identity (`%cn <%ce>`). A missing
sign-off, a sign-off naming someone other than the committer, a body or an extra trailer fails.
The `commit-msg` hook does not check this, so it surfaces only at `git push` (the `pre-push`
hook) or in CI `style_git`.
