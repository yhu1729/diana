Each VST-verified source `src/<source>.c` has one committed `clightgen` output
`verification/clight/<source>_<target>.v`. `<target>` is `<arch>_<system>`, or
`<arch>_<model>_<system>` when the model is not `default`, from `COMPCERT_ARCH`,
`COMPCERT_MODEL` and `COMPCERT_SYSTEM` in the `compcert.config` of the CompCert installation.

- The output depends on the target: its `Info` module records `arch`, `model`, `abi` (`"apple"`
  today), `bitsize`, `source_file` and the CompCert `version` (`"3.17"` from CompCert 3.18).
- A VST file imports its AST by module name, for example
  `From Diana.Clight Require Import bisection_mid_aarch64_macos.`, through
  `-Q ../clight Diana.Clight` in `verification/rocq/_CoqProject`.
- Today all 15 ASTs are `aarch64_macos`: `bisection_init`, `bisection_mid`, `bisection_result`,
  `bisection_step`, `euler_step`, `euler_step_unchecked`, `newton_init`, `newton_result`,
  `newton_step`, `rk4_stage`, `rk4_stage_unchecked`, `rk4_step`, `rk4_step_unchecked`,
  `simpson`, `trapezoidal`.
- Another target needs its own CompCert switch, its own committed ASTs (an x86-64 Linux build of
  CompCert has arch `x86`, model `64`, system `linux`, giving `<source>_x86_64_linux.v`) and VST
  files that import them by that name. No AST of another target exists.
- The `.vo`, `.glob` and similar files Rocq writes beside the ASTs are git-ignored.
