id: WP-003
title: The SKIP list in run_frama_c.sh is the trust boundary between WP and Flocq
kind: rule
path:
  - script/run_frama_c.sh
  - src/bisection_mid.h
  - src/bisection_mid.c
  - src/fp_sqrt.h
related: [WP-004, WP-005, PROOF-007, FP-006, CI-013]

`SKIP` at the top of `script/run_frama_c.sh` lists the only functions whose ACSL contracts WP may
assume without proving them. Every entry must be discharged outside WP: a layer-B (Flocq) theorem
proves the contract's float fact, and a VST theorem proves the C body against that model.

- Today `SKIP="diana_bisection_mid diana_fp_sqrt"`.
- `diana_bisection_mid`, contract on the declaration in `src/bisection_mid.h`, assumed at every
  call in `src/bisection_init.c`, `src/bisection_step.c` and `src/bisection_result.c`:
  BISECTION-B1 (`Diana.Float.Bisection.mid_in_bracket`) proves the rounded midpoint finite and
  inside `[a, b]`; BISECTION-C1 (`Diana.VST.BisectionMid.body_diana_bisection_mid`) proves the C
  body computes that midpoint.
- `diana_fp_sqrt`, contract in `src/fp_sqrt.h`, assumed at its call in `src/cholesky.c`:
  CHOLESKY-B1 (`Diana.Float.Cholesky.sqrt_contract`) proves the contract of Flocq's `Bsqrt`, and
  CHOLESKY-C2 (`Diana.VST.FpSqrt.body_diana_fp_sqrt`) that the body returns `Float.sqrt`, under
  an assumed funspec for `__builtin_sqrt` (FP-006).
- An entry has the shape of `diana_bisection_mid`: an external `diana_` function alone in its
  source file, its contract on the declaration in a private header, and no expectation row
  (WP-005).
- The CI Frama-C job assumes every `SKIP` contract. CI `verify_rocq` checks its layer-B theorem
  (CI-013), but only the local VST build proves the C body, so adding an entry adds to what a
  green pipeline does not establish.
- The script fails on any other contract WP would assume (WP-004), so the list cannot grow
  silently. It is an input of the report stamp (GATE-009), so a change to it fails CI
  `verify_provenance` until a local `script/dev.sh verify` has rewritten the report.
