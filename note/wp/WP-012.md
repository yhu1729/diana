---
id: WP-012
title: LU computes the pivot magnitude in line rather than with diana_fp_abs
kind: rationale
path:
  - src/lu.c
related: [WP-011, FP-002, API-032]
---
The pivot search of `src/lu.c` takes the magnitude as `v = lu[i + k*n]; if (v < 0.0) v = -v;`
instead of calling `diana_fp_abs`, the `src/fp.h` helper that bisection and Newton use.

- `diana_fp_abs` has the WP precondition `\is_finite(x)`. At the search of step `k` the column
  holds copies of `a` or checked updates, but proving that from a loop invariant needs either
  "every entry is finite", which fails for the multipliers (finite only because their magnitude
  is at most 1, a float fact Alt-Ergo does not prove from a division), or "the trailing block is
  finite", which is lost at each store into another column unless WP proves
  `i + j*n ≠ i' + j'*n`, a nonlinear disequality it does not prove.
- The in-line form has no precondition, so LU-C1 needs no finiteness at all; finiteness is
  LU-B1, and VST proves the C against the model whose magnitude is the same `v < 0 ? −v : v`
  (`fmag`, `c_mag`).
- It is plain IEEE arithmetic, not a libm call (FP-001), and its branch is covered by the tests.
  The Clight AST of `lu` has no `diana_fp_abs` copy, so its spec lists only the
  `diana_fp_finite` copy (VST-006).
